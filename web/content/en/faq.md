# FAQ

## Selling and distributing your works

### Can I sell my works on BOOTH?

Yes. You can sell finished works on BOOTH and similar platforms, as long as they use images that you own the rights to or have permission from the rights holder to use. You can sell them with a regular download. There is no separate commercial license to buy and no revenue share.

However, you can't sell templates without images, models with the images removed, or models where only the colors were changed. For details, see Article 6 of the [Terms of Service](/terms).

### Can I distribute my works for free?

Yes. You can distribute finished works for free under the same conditions as selling them. You can't distribute templates without images, models with the images removed, or models where only the colors were changed.

### What can people who buy (or receive) a finished work do?

They can use, place, and adjust the finished work in VRChat and similar platforms (for example, placing it in a world, adjusting its position, size, and rotation, or changing gimmick settings).

They can't redistribute or resell the package, or any models or textures extracted from it. These conditions are also written in the LICENSE.txt included in the package.

### Can I use the images I added elsewhere?

Yes, as long as you own the rights to them. The VRPrintLab terms don't restrict your rights to the original images or your use of them outside VRPrintLab.

## Tokens and downloads

### Do I need tokens to download the same work again?

Once you've downloaded something, you can download it again for free as many times as you like. Even if you upload the same image again, it's treated as the same content as long as the image itself and the edit settings are the same.

### Do I need tokens again after editing a work?

If you change how it looks (images, colors, placement, and so on), it counts as a new download and needs the tokens for the template. If you only add gimmicks without changing how it looks, you only need tokens for the added gimmicks. Changing gimmick settings or removing gimmicks doesn't need tokens. The number of tokens needed is shown before you download.

### Do tokens expire?

Yes, 180 days after they're purchased or granted. Tokens closest to their expiration date are used first.

### Can I get a refund?

As a rule, no. However, if you couldn't download because of a problem on our side, we'll refund your unused tokens and the amount affected by the problem.

## Saving and deleting data

### What happens to edits I make before logging in?

They're saved temporarily in your browser, and you can carry them over as a work when you log in. They can be carried over for 24 hours after they were saved.

### If I delete a work, will my downloads be deleted too?

No. Copies of your downloads (the package and copies of the original images) stay in your download history, and you can download them again. If you also want to delete these copies, delete them from the details page in your download history. Once deleted, they can't be downloaded again.

### What happens if I delete my account?

Your works, uploaded images, copies of your downloads, and remaining tokens are deleted, and you can no longer download them again. Payment records, token history, and download records (excluding images and packages) are kept as transaction records.
