## 1. Information we collect

To provide the Service, the Operator collects the following information:

- **Account information**: Email address, display name, and password (stored in a form that cannot be converted back to the original string; even the Operator cannot see it)
- **Information when logging in with an external service**: Information provided by Google or Discord, such as your email address, name, and an ID that identifies you
- **Images you upload and the edit settings of your works**
- **Token and download records**: History of token grants, purchases, use, and expiration, and records and copies of downloaded content
- **Payment information**: The amount, date and time, and type of payment method, the payment ID at our payment service (Stripe), and a value used to identify your card (not the card number itself). Card information such as card numbers is collected by Stripe; the Operator does not collect or store it
- **Inquiry details**: The content of your inquiry and your contact email address
- **Access records**: IP address, browser type, date and time of access, and so on (recorded by the server services that run the Service)
- **Phone number**: Collected if SMS verification is introduced for receiving the sign-up bonus 【TBD: when it will be introduced】

## 2. Purposes of use

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

1. Providing the Service (logging in, saving works, generating and downloading packages, and managing tokens)
2. Processing payments and handling refunds and payment reversals
3. Preventing fraudulent use (preventing the sign-up bonus from being received more than once and repeated payment reversals, including blocking cards whose payments have been reversed)
4. Responding to claims of rights infringement and to violations of the Terms of Service
5. Responding to inquiries and sending important notices about the Service
6. Investigating problems with and improving the Service

If we collect phone numbers, we use them only for purpose 3. Phone numbers that have received the sign-up bonus are stored, even after account deletion, in a form that cannot be converted back to the original number (a hash value).

## 5. Retention period and deletion

- Edits made in the editor before logging in are saved only in your browser and are not sent to the Operator's servers. Only edits saved within the last 24 hours can be carried over after logging in; expired edits are deleted the next time you open the editor.
- When you delete a work, the work and the images uploaded for editing it are deleted. Copies of downloaded content (including copies of the original images) are kept until you delete them from your download history or delete your account.
- When you delete your account, your works, images, and copies of downloaded content are deleted. Payment records, token history, and download records (excluding images and packages) are kept as transaction records for 【TBD: retention period】.
- Deleted data may remain in backups and access records for some time. 【TBD: retention period for backups and logs (items 2, 3, and 5)】

## 6. Security measures

To prevent leaks of personal information, the Operator takes the following measures:

- Encrypted communication (HTTPS)
- Per-user access restrictions in the database (settings that allow you to read and write only your own data)
- Storing uploaded images and downloadable files privately, and handing them over through time-limited links (1 hour for displaying images and 5 minutes for downloads)
- Limiting who can use the admin pages and recording administrator actions

Please note that a time-limited link can be opened by anyone who knows it while it is valid.

## 7. Use of cookies

The Service uses cookies to keep you logged in and to remember the language you choose. It also stores editor settings (such as whether to show confirmations) and edits made before logging in in your browser. We do not use cookies for analytics or advertising.
